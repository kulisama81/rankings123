# Tennis 4-week schedule & ranking projection (parity gap)

PARITY GAP: live-tennis.eu shows 'Rankings Projection for the next 4 weeks' and 'Tennis Players Schedule for the next 4 weeks'. We lack both. Users (especially bettors) need to see upcoming tournament schedules and how they'll affect rankings over the next month.

## Acceptance Criteria

New page or section: (1) 4-week tournament calendar (ATP/WTA), (2) Per-player: which tournaments they're entered in, (3) Ranking projection: if they perform at seed level, what's their rank in 4 weeks. Data source: ATP/WTA official calendars or ESPN. Verify: Can see Sinner's next 4 tournaments + projected rank movement.
