# Post-World Cup Final immediate retention & Tour de France pivot

## Notes

**2026-07-18T13:48:50Z**

**FIRST-PRINCIPLES ROI (Retention crisis - World Cup traffic spike ends July 19 evening):**

**Fundamental truth:** Retention = users return because something ELSE valuable exists beyond what brought them here.

**The crisis:** World Cup Final ends July 19 ~6PM ET. Analytics show /world-cup has 41.2% bounce (GOOD engagement during tournament) BUT homepage has 70.2% bounce. When the tournament ends, what keeps World Cup visitors from leaving forever?

**Current gap:** Homepage and /world-cup page have NO cross-sport discovery. A World Cup fan finishing the Final has no reason to explore tennis or cycling rankings.

**Opportunity window:** Tour de France is LIVE (through July 27) + Wimbledon just ended (July 12) so tennis rankings are fresh. We can pivot WC visitors to TdF + tennis if we surface it IMMEDIATELY after the Final.

**Impact vs Effort:**
- Impact: HIGH - captures 10-50% of WC traffic spike (57 views/28 days, likely 500+ views July 19) and converts them to multi-sport users
- Effort: MEDIUM - homepage hero swap, /world-cup 'What's Next' module, SEO pivot
- ROI: EXCEPTIONAL - compounds user LTV vs one-time WC visitor

**Implementation:**
1. Homepage pivot (ships July 19 at 6PM ET, right after Final ends):
   - Swap World Cup Final countdown for 'Relive the Final: Spain vs Argentina recap' + Tour de France live widget
   - Add 'What's Live Now' cross-sport module: TdF (stage X/21), ATP/WTA (upcoming tournaments)
2. /world-cup page pivot:
   - Hero changes from 'World Cup 2026 Final' to 'Final Recap: Spain [score] Argentina' + highlight video
   - Add 'What's Next in Sports' section: Tour de France GC standings, US Open preview (starts Aug 25), 2030 World Cup futures odds
3. SEO: Update meta descriptions from 'World Cup 2026 live' to 'World Cup 2026 Final Results + What's Next' to capture post-Final search traffic

**Time-sensitive:** Ships TONIGHT (July 18) so it goes live IMMEDIATELY after the Final whistle (July 19 ~6PM ET). Every hour delay loses WC visitors who won't return.

**ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA:**
1. Homepage hero swaps from WC Final countdown to Final recap + TdF live widget (triggered manually or auto at July 19 6PM ET)
