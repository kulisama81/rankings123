# Internal Linking Strategy — Cross-Link Rankings/Players/Tournaments

Systematic internal linking to boost SEO and engagement. Link player names → player pages, tournament mentions → tournament pages, related rankings (ATP Live ↔ WTA Live ↔ Race rankings). FIRST-PRINCIPLES: Internal links distribute PageRank, improve crawlability, increase pages/session. Wikipedia's strength = dense internal linking. ACCEPTANCE: Player names auto-link, 'Related' sections on pages, breadcrumbs, footer sitemap. ROI: 3-5 hours, boosts SEO + session depth.
