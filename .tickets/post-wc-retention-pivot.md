# Post-World Cup Final traffic retention strategy (July 20+ pivot plan)

## Notes

**2026-07-16T13:49:21Z**

**Context**: World Cup Final is July 19. The tournament ENDS that day, and World Cup traffic (currently 57 views, top page) will drop sharply. Need immediate pivot plan to retain visitors and redirect engagement.

**First-principles thinking**:
- User behavior shift: 'World Cup 2026 results' replaces 'World Cup 2026 live' searches post-Final
- Traffic retention: Convert World Cup visitors to tennis/cycling/multi-sport users before they leave
- Revenue continuity: Betting affiliates need new contexts (Olympics? Tennis majors? Tour de France ongoing until July 27?)
- Content decay: Time-sensitive World Cup content (countdown, live standings, betting picks) becomes stale instantly

**Immediate actions (ship by July 20):**
1. **World Cup Final recap article** - ship within hours of Final ending (results, highlights, champion celebration) with SEO for 'World Cup 2026 Final results'
2. **Homepage pivot** - replace 'World Cup Live' with 'Tour de France Live' (ongoing until July 27) as primary hero
3. **Cross-sport CTAs** - add 'What to watch next' module to World Cup pages: 'Tour de France in its final week', 'US Open 2026 coming soon', 'Paralympics 2026'
4. **Betting pivot** - shift affiliate context from World Cup to Tour de France stage winners, Olympics futures (if applicable), US Open pre-tournament odds
5. **Archive organization** - keep World Cup 2026 content accessible but clearly marked 'Final Results' (not live)
6. **Analytics baseline** - measure July 20-27 retention: how many World Cup visitors return for other sports?

**Longer-term (July 20-Aug 1):**
7. **Olympics 2026 prep** - if Paris Olympics start soon, build Olympics coverage BEFORE opening ceremony (not after)
8. **Tennis major cadence** - US Open 2026 dates? Start pre-tournament content 2 weeks before
9. **SEO content hub** - 'Best sports to bet on after World Cup' listicle, 'World Cup 2026 retrospective'

**Acceptance**:
- By July 20: Final recap live, homepage shows TdF as primary (not WC), cross-sport CTAs added
- Analytics: Track homepage bounce rate July 20-27 vs July 10-19 (expect drop if pivot works)
- Revenue: Tour de France betting context added to at least 3 stage pages
- Post-WC ticket post-wc-content-pivot (currently p2) is either executed or refined based on this plan
