# Tennis Live Streaks & Form Indicators

Surface current form at a glance: win/loss streaks (W5, L3), recent tournament results, hard/clay/grass splits. ESPN/SofaScore show this; traditional ranking sites don't. Differentiates us as a stats-forward product.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Each player row shows recent form: W/L last 5 matches as colored badges. 2. Hover/expand: last 3 tournament results with round reached. 3. Surface split: W-L on hard/clay/grass this year. 4. Data from ESPN matches API. 5. Works on ATP Live, WTA Live, player pages.
