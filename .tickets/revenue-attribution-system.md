# Revenue Attribution System — Track What Content Earns Money

Build attribution to understand which content drives revenue. FIRST-PRINCIPLES: Can't optimize what you don't measure. Need to know: (1) Which pages drive affiliate clicks, (2) Which traffic sources convert to revenue (organic vs direct vs social), (3) Which content topics earn most (betting vs rankings vs tournament coverage). Use GA4 + affiliate tracking pixels. Measure: Page → Affiliate Click → Conversion. Acceptance: Implement affiliate click tracking, integrate with GA4, create revenue attribution report showing top-earning pages, traffic sources, and content types. Use to inform content prioritization.
