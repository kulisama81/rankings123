# Brand positioning foundation document

Per Clay "AI Is Changing Branding" (July 22, 2026): "Speed without direction produces polished noise." Before scaling AI-generated assets (social cards, OG images, ads), document the strategic foundation: 1) What's our unique positioning vs competitors? 2) Who's our core audience and what emotional response do we want? 3) What's our brand personality (authoritative vs enthusiastic vs analytical)?

Rankings123 has good visual systems (design tokens, typography, per-sport accents) but lacks documented strategic positioning. This creates risk: AI tools can rapidly generate brand assets, but without clear strategy they'll be polished but generic. Need the north star BEFORE accelerating execution.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Create `docs/BRAND-STRATEGY.md` with 4 sections: Positioning (what makes us different), Audience (who + emotional response), Personality (voice/tone), Visual Expression (how strategy manifests in design)
- Positioning statement (1-2 sentences): "Rankings123 is [what] for [who] that [unique value]"
- Audience profile: sports fan archetype (casual vs hardcore, mobile-first behavior, what they value)
- Personality: 3-5 brand attributes with do/don't examples (e.g. "Confident, not arrogant: DO show live data authority; DON'T use ALL CAPS hype")
- Visual expression: map personality to design choices (bold Archivo = confident authority; per-sport accents = celebration of each sport's identity)
- Cross-reference DESIGN-IDENTITY.md (visual system) — strategy drives identity, identity manifests strategy
- Review existing competitor positioning (live-tennis.eu, FlashScore, SofaScore) — what gap do we fill?
