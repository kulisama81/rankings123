# Betting affiliate content strategy: where to place links for maximum conversion

Betting affiliates offer 5-10× higher RPM than AdSense ($50-100 vs $9-18), but ONLY if affiliate links are placed strategically where users have BUYING INTENT. Research shows buyer-intent queries like 'sportsbook reviews' and 'best odds' convert at 5-8%. Document strategy: which pages get affiliate links? (match previews, betting guides, odds comparisons, tournament predictions). Where on page? (above fold, in odds tables, call-to-action buttons). What NOT to do? (no intrusive popups, no fake urgency, never fabricate odds).

## Acceptance Criteria

Strategy document: docs/betting-affiliate-strategy.md, High-conversion page types identified (predictions, betting guides, odds comparisons), Placement guidelines (above fold, contextual, non-intrusive), Content requirements (must have REAL odds from API, not placeholders), CTA copy that converts (data-driven from affiliate best practices), Anti-patterns documented (no fake urgency, no popups, CX-first rule enforced), Ticket created for first betting guide implementation

**FIRST-PRINCIPLES ROI (High-Intent Placement = High Conversion):**

Fundamental truth: Affiliate revenue = Clicks × Conversion Rate × Commission.

Conversion rate depends on USER INTENT:
- Low intent: Rankings page user → 0.5-1% conversion (just checking rank)
- Medium intent: Match preview reader → 2-3% conversion (considering betting)
- High intent: 'Best odds' searcher → 5-8% conversion (ready to bet NOW)

Where to place affiliate links (priority order):
1. **Betting guides** (8% conversion) - "Cincinnati Open 2026 Betting Guide"
2. **Odds comparison pages** (5-7% conversion) - "Sinner vs Alcaraz odds comparison"
3. **Tournament predictions** (3-5% conversion) - "US Open 2026 Predictions"
4. **Match previews** (2-3% conversion) - "Semifinal preview: Spain vs France"
5. **Rankings pages** (0.5-1% conversion) - contextual, not primary CTA

CX-FIRST enforcement:
- ❌ NEVER show affiliate links without REAL odds (placeholder = trust killer)
- ❌ NEVER use fake urgency ("Bet now! Offer ends in 5 min!" when it doesn't)
- ❌ NEVER intrusive popups (kills user experience)
- ✅ ALWAYS disclose affiliate relationship (FTC compliance)
- ✅ ALWAYS provide value FIRST (analysis, insights, odds comparison)

Effort: LOW (3-4 hours to document strategy)
Impact: CRITICAL (difference between 1% and 7% conversion = 7× revenue on same traffic)
